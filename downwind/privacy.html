Downwind Privacy Policy

Downwind: Airport Traffic, for iPhone, iPad and Mac, by LD Clinton. Last updated 18 November 2026.

The short version

Downwind has no account, advertising, analytics or tracking. LD Clinton does not collect, receive, sell or share any information about you. Everything you record or save stays on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Your location, used for the map, Sky View and the flight recorder.
  • Recorded flights, imported track files and their debriefs.
  • Your spotting log and its photos, your watchlist, and your settings.
  • API keys and logins you add for traffic services, kept in your device's keychain.

If you use iCloud backup, your device's backup may include this data, under Apple's privacy policy.

Requests to outside services

To show live traffic, weather and aircraft details, Downwind asks public services over the internet. Each request carries only what is needed to answer it:

  • Live traffic (adsb.lol, adsb.fi, or another source you choose in Settings): the area shown on the map, your position for Sky View, or the area around a home point you set for alerts.
  • Aircraft details, routes and photos (adsbdb.com, airport-data.com, The OpenSky Network): an aircraft's transponder code, tail number or callsign.
  • Weather and airport data (aviationweather.gov, U.S. FAA, Iowa Environmental Mesonet): an airport identifier or map area.
  • Data updates (lazlaru.github.io): a daily check for newer FAA airport and aircraft data. Nothing about you is sent.

These services receive your device's internet address, as any website does. Their own privacy policies apply to what they receive. Downwind doesn't send your name, contacts, recorded flights or photos to any of them.

Pages you open in Downwind's web panel or your browser, such as FlightAware, the FAA's registry and LiveATC.net, are those sites' own pages, with their own cookies and advertising.

Permissions

  • Location, including in the background while you record a flight, for the flight debrief and the map.
  • Camera, for Sky View and reading a tail number. Nothing is recorded or sent.
  • Motion (barometer), to record pressure altitude during a flight.
  • Local network, to receive traffic and GPS from your ADS-B receiver.
  • Photos (add only), when you save a sighting card.
  • Notifications, for watchlist alerts.

You can turn any of these off in your device's Settings.

Children

Downwind doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue on GitHub, or see the Downwind support page.
